## FAQ Section

### Section Headline
**Frequently Asked Questions**

### Q1: How is this different from Canva or other logo makers?
We start with your STORY, not just aesthetics. Other tools ask "what colors do you like?" We ask "why does your business exist?" The result is a brand that has meaning and helps you communicate your mission.

### Q2: Do I need design experience?
Zero. If you can fill out a form and click buttons, you can use BrandStory. We handle all the design complexity.

### Q3: How long does it take?
Most founders complete their brand in 15-20 minutes. The AI interview takes 5-10 minutes, then you get instant results.

### Q4: What if I don't like the designs?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. But 95% of our customers find a logo they love in their first set of 10 options.

### Q5: Can I trademark my logo?
Yes! You own full commercial rights. We also check for obvious conflicts, but we recommend hiring a trademark attorney for official registration (we can refer you to partners).

### Q6: What file formats do I get?
Everything you need: PNG (transparent background), SVG (scalable vector), PDF (print-ready), and AI (Adobe Illustrator) files.

### Q7: Is this really AI or do humans design it?
It's AI-powered generation based on your story, trained on thousands of professional designs. For an additional $99, you can have a human designer refine your chosen concept.

### Q8: What makes you "mission-driven startup" focused?
We understand the unique challenges of social impact businesses. Our AI is trained on impact sector aesthetics, and we help you communicate your mission, not just look pretty.
