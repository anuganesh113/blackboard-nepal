## 📊 Key Metrics to Track

### Pre-Launch
- **Interviews Completed**: Target 10+
- **Concept Approval Rate**: Target 70%+
- **Willingness to Pay**: Target 50%+

### Post-Launch
- **Sign-ups**: Track daily/weekly
- **Active Users**: DAU/WAU/MAU
- **Conversion Rate**: Free → Paid (Target 5%+)
- **Retention**: D7 (Target 40%+), D30 (Target 20%+)
- **CAC**: Keep under LTV/3
- **Churn**: Keep under 5% monthly

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: How do I know if my idea is good?
**A**: If 70%+ of potential users confirm the problem and 50%+ are willing to pay, you have validation.

### Q: Should I build everything myself?
**A**: Start with MVP. You can always add features later. Focus on core value first.

### Q: How much should I charge?
**A**: Research competitors, test with users, ensure LTV:CAC ratio is 3:1 or better.

### Q: What if competitors copy me?
**A**: Focus on execution, build community, create switching costs, move fast.

### Q: How long until I see revenue?
**A**: Typically 3-6 months from start to first paying customers, but varies widely.

### Q: Do I need funding?
**A**: Depends on your model. Many SaaS products can bootstrap to $10K MRR.
