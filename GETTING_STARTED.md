## 🎤 Quick User Interview Guide

### Where to Find People:
1. **Reddit**: Post in r/entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness, r/restaurantowners (if restaurant-focused), etc.
2. **LinkedIn**: Post asking for 15-min interviews, message connections
3. **Facebook Groups**: Join relevant groups, ask for help
4. **Twitter/X**: Post asking for interviews
5. **Local**: Coffee shops, coworking spaces, business meetups

### Your Interview Script (15 minutes):

**Opening (2 min)**:
- "Thanks for your time! I'm building [product name] and want to understand your needs better."

**Problem Discovery (5 min)**:
1. "Tell me about your experience with [problem area]"
2. "What's your current process for [related task]?"
3. "What's the most frustrating part?"

**Solution Validation (5 min)**:
4. "If I showed you a solution that [your value prop], would you use it?"
5. "How much would you pay for this?"
6. "What features are most important?"

**Closing (3 min)**:
7. "Who else do you know who has this problem?"
8. "Would you be interested in beta testing?"

**Action**: Schedule your first interview TODAY!

## 💡 If You're Stuck...

### "I can't decide which idea to pick"
→ **Solution**: Pick the one you can talk about for 10 minutes without notes. That's your passion.

### "I don't know my target audience well enough"
→ **Solution**: That's what interviews are for! Pick a direction, then learn through conversations.

### "I'm not sure if my idea is unique enough"
→ **Solution**: Start with validation. If users confirm the problem and want your solution, uniqueness will come from execution.

### "I want to build but haven't validated yet"
→ **Solution**: Don't! Validation saves you months of building the wrong thing. Do 5 interviews first (minimum).
