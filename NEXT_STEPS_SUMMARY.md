## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Don't overthink** - Pick an idea and start validating
2. **Talk to users early** - Don't wait until you have everything figured out
3. **Start small** - MVP means MINIMUM viable product
4. **Iterate quickly** - Ship, learn, improve, repeat
5. **Focus on one niche** - Don't try to serve everyone

---

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Building before validating** - Talk to users first!  
❌ **Trying to serve everyone** - Pick a niche  
❌ **Feature creep** - Stick to MVP  
❌ **Perfectionism** - Done is better than perfect  
❌ **Ignoring competitors** - Know your competition
