# Brand Questionnaire - Story-Driven Design

## Purpose
This questionnaire helps us understand your brand story so we can create logos that visually represent your mission, values, and purpose.

---

### Q1: What's your business name?
**Field Type:** Text input
**Placeholder:** e.g., "GreenPath Education"
**Why we ask:** Obviously needed for the logo text
**AI Use:** Direct text in logo generation

## SECTION 2: The Story (Core Questions)

### Q3: Why did you start this business? What problem keeps you up at night?
**Field Type:** Long text area (500 char max)
**Placeholder:** "I saw too many kids in my community without access to quality education. I started this because..."
**Prompt:** Tell us the real story - the moment you knew you had to do this.

**Why we ask:** Uncovers the emotional core and primary motivation
**AI Use:** Identifies key themes (education access, community, empowerment, etc.) to translate into visual metaphors

**Example Answers:**
- "I watched my grandmother struggle to get healthcare in rural areas. No one should have to choose between their health and a 3-hour drive."
- "Every year, 8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans. I couldn't just watch anymore."
- "I grew up food insecure. Now I'm building a system so no kid goes to school hungry."

---

### Q4: If your organization didn't exist, what would the world lose?
**Field Type:** Long text area (300 char max)
**Placeholder:** "Communities would lose access to..."
**Prompt:** Think big. What gap would remain unfilled?

**Why we ask:** Reveals unique value proposition and impact
**AI Use:** Identifies transformation themes (darkness → light, isolation → connection, scarcity → abundance)

**Example Answers:**
- "A bridge between traditional medicine and modern healthcare for indigenous communities"
- "The only free coding education for incarcerated individuals"
- "Safe spaces for LGBTQ+ youth in conservative rural areas"

---

### Q16: Avoid these elements
**Field Type:** Text area (optional)
**Placeholder:** "No religious symbols, no animals, avoid red color..."
**Prompt:** Any deal-breakers or things to avoid?

**Why we ask:** Respects cultural/personal boundaries
**AI Use:** Filters out inappropriate elements

**Common Avoidances:**
- Religious symbols (if secular org)
- Certain colors (cultural meanings)
- Clichés (lightbulbs for ideas, globes for global)
- Gendered imagery
- Animals (some cultures/beliefs)

### AI Prompt Construction

**Template for generating logo prompts:**

```
Create a professional logo design for {businessName}, a {industry} organization.

BRAND STORY:
Origin: {originStory}
Impact: {worldWithout}
Transformation: {transformation.from} → {transformation.to}

BRAND PERSONALITY: {brandPersonality[0]}, {brandPersonality[1]}, {brandPersonality[2]}

VISUAL DIRECTION:
Style: {visualStyle}
Feeling: {targetFeeling}
Colors: {colorPreference}
Symbols: {symbolSuggestions}

REQUIREMENTS:
- Must work on: {primaryUsage}
- Appeal to: {primaryAudience}
- Differentiation: {differentiation}
- Logo type: {logoType}
- Avoid: {avoidElements}

Generate a {logoType} logo that visually represents the transformation from {transformation.from} to {transformation.to}, 
feeling {targetFeeling}, with a {visualStyle} aesthetic.

Style: vector art, professional, scalable, simple, memorable
```

### AI Generation Parameters

**DALL-E 3 Settings:**
- Size: 1024x1024 (square)
- Quality: "hd"
- Style: "vivid" for bold brands, "natural" for organic brands
- Negative prompts: "photograph, 3D render, realistic texture, gradient"

**Generate Multiple Variations:**
- 10 different prompts based on story
- Variation 1-3: Emphasize transformation metaphor
- Variation 4-6: Emphasize personality words
- Variation 7-8: Emphasize feeling/emotion
- Variation 9-10: Abstract interpretations
