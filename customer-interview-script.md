# Customer Interview Script
## Validating BrandStory AI (or any branding tool concept)

**Goal:** Understand pain points, validate willingness to pay, refine product concept
**Target:** 10-20 interviews before building
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes each
**Format:** Video call preferred (Zoom, Google Meet)

---

## PREPARATION (Before the Call)

### Research Your Interviewee (5 minutes)
- [ ] Check their LinkedIn profile
- [ ] Visit their company website/social media
- [ ] Look at their current branding (logo, colors)
- [ ] Note when they founded their business
- [ ] Identify their industry/sector

### Set Up
- [ ] Recording tool ready (with permission)
- [ ] Note-taking doc open
- [ ] Have examples of similar tools ready (Canva, Looka, etc.)
- [ ] Prepare to screen-share if needed

---

## OPENING (2 minutes)

### Introduction
"Hi [Name], thanks so much for making time. I'm [Your Name], and I'm researching how entrepreneurs approach branding for their businesses. This isn't a sales call - I genuinely want to learn from your experience.

I'll ask about your branding journey, what worked, what didn't, and get your thoughts on a concept I'm exploring. Should take about 15-20 minutes. Sound good?"

### Permission
"Quick housekeeping - do you mind if I record this? It's just so I can focus on our conversation instead of furiously typing notes. I won't share the recording publicly, just use it for my research."

**[Wait for permission before recording]**

### Set Expectations
"There are no right or wrong answers. I'm here to learn, not pitch. Feel free to be brutally honest - that's actually the most helpful feedback I can get."

---

## PART 1: Their Story (5 minutes)

### Opening Questions

**Q1: Tell me about [Business Name]. What do you do and who do you serve?**

*[Listen for: Their passion, clarity of mission, target audience]*

**Follow-up probes:**
- "What made you start this?"
- "What problem keeps you up at night?"

---

**Q2: When did you launch? What stage are you at?**

*[Listen for: Timeline, funding, team size, growth stage]*

**Context to gather:**
- Pre-launch / Just launched / 1-2 years / Established (3+ years)
- Solo founder / Small team / Larger team
- Bootstrapped / Funded
- Revenue status (pre-revenue / early revenue / scaling)

---

## PART 2: Branding Experience (8 minutes)

### The Core Questions

**Q3: Walk me through how you created your current brand/logo. Start from the beginning.**

*[This is the GOLDEN question. Let them talk. Don't interrupt. Listen for pain points, frustrations, costs, time invested]*

**Listen for:**
- DIY vs hired someone
- Tools used (if DIY)
- Cost (if hired)
- Timeline
- Satisfaction level
- Iterations/changes
- Emotional language (frustration, excitement, overwhelm)

**Probing questions (as needed):**
- "How long did that process take?"
- "What was the hardest part?"
- "How much did you end up spending?"
- "If you could do it again, what would you change?"

---

**Q4: On a scale of 1-10, how happy are you with your current branding?**

*[Get a number, then dig into why]*

**If 7 or below:**
- "What would make it a 10?"
- "What's holding you back from changing it?"
- "What specifically don't you like about it?"

**If 8+:**
- "What makes it work so well?"
- "Was there a moment when you knew you had the right brand?"
- "How long did it take to get to this point?"

---

**Q5: Beyond the logo itself, what other branding challenges did you face?**

*[Listen for: Consistency, guidelines, applying brand across different materials, brand story articulation]*

**Probing questions:**
- "Did you create brand guidelines?"
- "How do you ensure consistency across different materials?"
- "Do you feel like your brand tells your story?"
- "Have you struggled to explain your brand to others?"

---

**Q6: If you were starting today, would you do anything differently with your branding?**

*[This reveals ideal solutions and unmet needs]*

---

## PART 3: Solution Validation (4 minutes)

### Introduce the Concept

**Setup:**
"Thanks for sharing all that. Based on conversations like this, I'm exploring a concept and would love your reaction.

Imagine a tool that starts with your *story* - your mission, values, why you exist - and then generates logo designs that visually represent that story. Not just 'pick your favorite colors,' but 'tell us what transformation you create in the world' and the AI translates that into visual metaphors.

You'd also get a written brand narrative document alongside the logo - something you could use for your website, pitch deck, etc."

**Pause - let them absorb**

---

### Get Reactions

**Q7: What's your initial reaction to that concept?**

*[Listen for: Interest level, skepticism, excitement, confusion]*

**Don't defend or explain yet. Let them process.**

---

**Q8: How is that different from tools you've used or considered?**

*[Validates uniqueness of positioning]*

---

**Q9: What would make you hesitant to use something like this?**

*[Reveals objections you'll need to overcome]*

**Common objections:**
- "I need something totally custom"
- "AI can't understand my mission"
- "I don't trust AI for something this important"
- "I've already spent money on branding"
- "Sounds complicated"
- "Probably too expensive"

**Probe deeper:**
- "What if [address concern]...?"
- "How would I need to prove [X] to you?"

---

**Q10: If this tool existed today, priced at $149, would you use it?**

*[This is the money question - literally]*

**If YES:**
- "What would you use it for specifically?"
- "When would you use it? (timing)"
- "What would make it worth $149 to you?"
- "Would you pay more for [additional feature]?"

**If NO:**
- "What price would feel right?" (don't suggest numbers, let them say)
- "What would need to be included to justify the cost?"
- "Is it the price, or something else holding you back?"

**If MAYBE:**
- "What would turn that maybe into a yes?"
- "What questions would you need answered first?"

---

**Q11: Who else do you know who would benefit from something like this?**

*[Market validation + potential referrals]*

---

## PART 4: Feature Prioritization (3 minutes)

**Q12: If you were using this tool, which of these would matter most to you?**

*[Read the list, have them rank top 3]*

**Options:**
A. Speed (get your brand in 10 minutes)
B. Customization (lots of editing control)
C. Story/meaning (understanding *why* your logo looks this way)
D. Complete kit (business cards, social media assets, etc.)
E. Brand guidelines document
F. Trademark screening (avoid legal issues)
G. Collaboration features (get team feedback)
H. Human designer refinement (hybrid AI + human)
I. Educational (learning design principles as you go)
J. Other: [let them suggest]

**Ask:** "Why those three?"

*[Reveals what they value most]*

---

**Q13: What would make you trust this tool enough to actually launch your business with this brand?**

*[Addresses trust barriers]*

**Probing:**
- Money-back guarantee?
- See examples from similar businesses?
- Free trial/preview?
- Reviews/testimonials?
- Human support available?
- Option to have designer review it?

---

## CLOSING (2 minutes)

### Appreciation
"This has been incredibly helpful. Your insights are going to directly shape what I build - or whether I build it at all."

### Next Steps

**If they showed strong interest:**
"I'm going to take everything I'm learning and build a prototype in the next few weeks. Would you be interested in being one of the first people to try it? No pressure - and I'd give you [early bird pricing / free access / discount] in exchange for feedback."

**[Get their email if interested]**

---

**If they were lukewarm:**
"Based on what you shared, it sounds like [this feature / this price / this approach] isn't quite right for you. That's really useful to know. Is there anyone in your network who might have different needs - maybe earlier stage founders or in a different industry?"

**[Ask for referrals]**

---

### Final Question

**Q14: Is there anything I didn't ask about that you think I should know?**

*[Often reveals gold]*

---

### Thank You
"Seriously, thank you. Can I send you a coffee gift card as a thank you for your time?"

**[Optional but builds goodwill]**

---

## POST-INTERVIEW (Immediately After)

### Debrief Notes (5 minutes)

**Rate the interview:**
- **Pain level:** How much pain do they have around branding? (1-10)
- **Interest level:** How interested were they in the solution? (1-10)
- **Willingness to pay:** Would they actually pay? (Yes/No/Maybe)
- **Urgency:** How soon would they use it? (Now/Soon/Someday)

**Key quotes:**
Write down 2-3 quotes that were particularly insightful

**Surprises:**
What did they say that you didn't expect?

**Pattern matching:**
After 5+ interviews, what themes are emerging?

## DECISION FRAMEWORK

### After 10 interviews, answer these:

**1. Is there real pain?**
- [ ] YES - 7+ people expressed significant frustration with current solutions
- [ ] MAYBE - Some pain but not urgent
- [ ] NO - People seem fine with current options

**2. Is there willingness to pay?**
- [ ] YES - 5+ people said they'd pay $149+
- [ ] MAYBE - People interested but price-sensitive
- [ ] NO - Most said they'd want it free or very cheap

**3. Is the solution compelling?**
- [ ] YES - 7+ people got excited about the concept
- [ ] MAYBE - Some interest but also skepticism
- [ ] NO - Most were lukewarm or confused

**4. Can you reach these customers?**
- [ ] YES - They're in identifiable communities/channels
- [ ] MAYBE - Scattered, need to figure out channels
- [ ] NO - No clear way to reach them

**5. Is the market big enough?**
- [ ] YES - Hundreds of thousands of potential customers
- [ ] MAYBE - Niche but potentially viable
- [ ] NO - Too small to build a business

---

## ACTION BASED ON RESULTS

### If 4-5 YES: BUILD IT
Strong validation. Move to MVP development.

### If 3 YES: BUILD WITH ADJUSTMENTS
Validation is there but refine:
- Adjust pricing
- Change feature priorities
- Narrow/broaden target market
- Clarify positioning

### If 0-2 YES: PIVOT OR STOP
Not enough validation. Either:
- **Pivot:** Talk to different customer segment
- **Refine:** Dramatically change the approach
- **Stop:** Save your time and money

---

## INTERVIEW TIPS & BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule)
✅ Ask "why" and "tell me more" frequently
✅ Dig into specific stories and examples
✅ Stay curious and non-defensive
✅ Take detailed notes
✅ Send thank you follow-up email

### DON'T:
❌ Pitch your solution before understanding their problem
❌ Lead the witness ("Wouldn't you love a tool that...")
❌ Defend your idea when they express concerns
❌ Talk over them or rush through
❌ Skip the money question (it's awkward but essential)
❌ Only talk to friends/family (they'll lie to be nice)
