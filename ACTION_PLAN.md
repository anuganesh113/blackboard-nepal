## 🎤 User Interview Quick Start

### Where to Find Users:
1. **Reddit**: Relevant subreddits (r/entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness, etc.)
2. **Facebook Groups**: Industry-specific groups
3. **LinkedIn**: Post asking for 15-min interviews
4. **Twitter/X**: Reach out to your target audience
5. **Local Communities**: Coffee shops, coworking spaces
6. **UserInterviews.com**: Paid platform (if budget allows)

### Quick Interview Script:
```
1. "Tell me about your experience with [problem area]"
2. "What's your current process for [related task]?"
3. "What's the most frustrating part?"
4. "If I showed you a solution that [your value prop], would you use it?"
5. "How much would you pay for this?"
6. "What features are most important?"
```

**Action**: Schedule your first 3 interviews TODAY

## 📊 Validation Checklist (Do Before Building)

### Must Complete:
- [ ] **10 user interviews** (minimum)
- [ ] **70%+ confirm problem exists**
- [ ] **50%+ willing to pay**
- [ ] **Mockups created and tested**
- [ ] **Unit economics calculated** (LTV:CAC > 3:1)

### Quick Validation Methods:
1. **Landing Page Test**: Create landing page, drive traffic, measure sign-ups
2. **Pre-Order Campaign**: Offer discount for early adopters
3. **Email List**: Build list before building product
4. **Concierge MVP**: Manually provide service to validate demand
