## 3. COST STRUCTURE

### Variable Costs (Per Customer)

```
AI Generation:
- DALL-E 3 (10 images @ $0.04): $0.40
- GPT-4 (explanations, brand story): $0.08
- Total AI: $0.48

Infrastructure:
- S3 storage (50MB): $0.001
- Bandwidth: $0.02
- Total Infrastructure: $0.02

Payment Processing:
- Stripe: $149 × 2.9% + $0.30 = $4.62

Email:
- Transactional emails (3): $0.01

Total Variable Cost: $5.13 per customer
Gross Margin: $149 - $5.13 = $143.87 (96.6%)
```

**With Upsells:**
- Average sale: $200
- Variable costs: ~$7 (including designer fees)
- Gross margin: $193 (96.5%)

### Break-Even Analysis

**Scenario 1: Bootstrapped (No paid ads)**
- Fixed costs: $5,000/year
- Contribution margin: $144/customer
- Break-even: 35 customers/year (3/month)

**Scenario 2: Moderate Marketing**
- Fixed costs: $15,000/year
- CAC: $30 (organic + paid mix)
- Contribution margin: $144 - $30 = $114
- Break-even: 132 customers/year (11/month)

**Scenario 3: Aggressive Growth**
- Fixed costs: $50,000/year (team, ads)
- CAC: $50
- Contribution margin: $144 - $50 = $94
- Break-even: 532 customers/year (44/month)

## 8. RISK ANALYSIS

### Risks & Mitigation

**Risk 1: AI Quality Issues**
- *Impact:* High refund rate, bad reviews
- *Likelihood:* Medium
- *Mitigation:* 
  - Extensive testing before launch
  - Money-back guarantee
  - Human review option
  - Continuous prompt improvement

**Risk 2: Low Conversion Rates**
- *Impact:* High CAC, unsustainable growth
- *Likelihood:* Medium
- *Mitigation:*
  - A/B test landing pages
  - Customer interviews to understand barriers
  - Free preview/trial version
  - Social proof and testimonials

**Risk 3: Competitor Copies Idea**
- *Impact:* Lost market share
- *Likelihood:* High (eventually)
- *Mitigation:*
  - Move fast, establish brand
  - Build community moat
  - Continuous innovation
  - Focus on niche where we're experts

**Risk 4: OpenAI API Changes/Pricing**
- *Impact:* Margin compression or product breaks
- *Likelihood:* Medium
- *Mitigation:*
  - Multi-model strategy (use Stable Diffusion backup)
  - Price buffer (AI costs are tiny part of price)
  - Monitor OpenAI announcements closely

**Risk 5: Payment Processing Issues**
- *Impact:* Lost revenue, customer frustration
- *Likelihood:* Low
- *Mitigation:*
  - Use reliable provider (Stripe)
  - Test extensively
  - Have backup payment method
  - Clear error messages
